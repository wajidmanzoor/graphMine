# Your saved work and deletion

Understand which copies of your work are stored and what the deletion controls remove.

GraphMine stores conversations, uploaded inputs, saved analyses and their input snapshots, reusable datasets, and exploration settings. Project snapshots and annotations are stored separately when you share them. A private conversation is not automatically visible to project members.

## How long is work kept?

In the current app, conversations are not automatically removed after a fixed inactivity period. They remain until deleted. Unused query previews expire after 24 hours. Read-only sharing links expire at the time chosen when they are created; expiry of a link does not delete the underlying project snapshot.

Separate review and diagnostic records can outlive a deleted conversation. Their retention period is not shown in the app. Contact the person who gave you access if you need a complete retention answer or removal beyond the workspace controls. Deleting a conversation should not be interpreted as erasing every separately retained record.

## Delete your own work

- In the conversation sidebar, open the conversation's action menu and choose **Delete**. The confirmation covers that conversation's uploads, results, and history. **Archive** only hides it from the default list.
- In **Data**, select an uploaded input and use its remove control. A completed analysis can still retain the immutable snapshot it used.
- Remove saved dataset items and versions through their library controls when no longer needed.
- Remove shared snapshots or projects through the project's controls if you own them. Revoke read-only links separately when appropriate.

Downloaded files are copies on the recipient's device. Removing a saved item or revoking a link does not delete a copy someone already downloaded.
