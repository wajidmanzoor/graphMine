# Allowances and waiting

Check your remaining allowance and understand why a question may need to wait.

Open **Settings › Account** to see your current AI allowance, usage, reset time, calculation allowance, and storage. Values belong to your account; examples in documentation are not your quota.

Input and generated tokens count toward the AI allowance. The allowance renews on the reset shown in the UI; signing out does not reset it. A shared allowance can also be exhausted before your individual allowance. Wait for the displayed reset or contact the person who gave you access.

## One calculation at a time

Let the current calculation finish before starting another in the same working conversation. Use **Activity** to distinguish queued work, model processing, and graph computation. A slow response does not mean the same question should be submitted repeatedly.

“The model is busy” means a language-model request cannot be accepted yet. Wait, then retry once the current work or queue has cleared. Opening more tabs does not increase your allowance. If the message persists, keep the visible error and contact the person who invited you.

## Storage

Saved uploads, analyses, dataset versions, project snapshots, and exploration settings can count toward storage. Remove work you no longer need using its own controls. Deleting one source file does not remove every independent saved or shared copy. Unused query previews expire after 24 hours; see [privacy](privacy.md) for the other retention boundaries.
